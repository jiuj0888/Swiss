Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the current site actually processes and the choices available to you.

Effective and last updated: August 5, 2026

1. Operator and contact point

Swiss Fare Check is an independent website for Swiss itinerary planning and transport pass and point-to-point fare comparison.

Privacy contact: Swiss Fare Check privacy enquiries
Email: jiuj0888@gmail.com

2. Information currently processed

  • Calculator inputs: travel date, travel days, passenger counts, selected routes and passes, manually entered fares and options
  • Email enquiries: sender email address, any name supplied, message content and attachments
  • Basic access data: hosting and network providers may process IP address, browser details, request time, requested URL and error records to deliver and secure the site.
Important: the current site code does not transmit calculator inputs to an operator-controlled server. Calculations and saved state remain in your browser.

3. Browser local storage

The site uses the swiss_fare_check_state_v1 local-storage key to restore selected routes, entered fares, travel date, passenger counts and pass options.

This information is not sent to the operator and may remain on your device until you clear this site's data or local storage.

4. Email enquiry data

We use your email address and message to answer enquiries, investigate reports and follow up on errors. Ordinary enquiries are retained for up to one year after the final response, then deleted, unless an ongoing investigation or legal obligation requires longer retention.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current repository does not include Google Analytics, Google AdSense or comparable third-party analytics or advertising scripts, and the site does not currently set advertising cookies.

If analytics or advertising is introduced later, this policy will be updated before activation and a consent mechanism will be provided where required by law or platform policy.

6. Purposes

  • Provide fare calculation, itinerary recommendation and state restoration
  • Respond to enquiries and error reports
  • Deliver, troubleshoot and secure the site

7. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information and do not disclose it to third parties except where required by law or separately authorised by you.

Email is received and stored through Google's Gmail service and may be processed outside your country as part of that service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Hosting and network providers may also process basic access logs for delivery and security.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local storage: until you clear browser data
  • Ordinary email enquiries: up to one year after the final response
  • Hosting logs not separately retained by the operator: according to provider settings and policy

Emails and attachments are deleted using account deletion tools when their retention purpose ends. Residual backups are removed according to the provider's backup cycle.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of email enquiry data relating to you. Email the address above and we will respond after reasonable identity checks. Because calculator state is stored only in your browser, you must clear it through your browser settings.

10. Children

We do not provide accounts or child profiles. Passenger age counts are processed only in your browser for fare calculation. Children should seek help from a parent or guardian before sending email.

11. Security

The site uses HTTPS, keeps calculator inputs in the browser, applies security attributes to external links and limits access to the repository and contact account.

12. External links

Third-party sites such as SBB, transport operators, Google and Instagram apply their own policies. Calculator inputs are not passed to them merely because a link appears on this site.

13. Policy changes

When processing changes, we will revise this page and its effective date. Material additions such as analytics or advertising will be documented and any required consent flow prepared before activation.

14. Contact and complaints

Privacy requests: jiuj0888@gmail.com. Depending on your location, you may also contact your local data-protection authority.
