How to use the planner
  1. Enter the number and ages of travellers and your travel date.
  2. For itinerary ideas, enter your duration, season and travel style in Route Planner.
  3. Compare the top three routes and their suggested pass costs.
  4. For an existing itinerary, choose the routes in Compare My Route.
  5. For more precise current prices, enter official point-to-point fares in Manual Calculator.
